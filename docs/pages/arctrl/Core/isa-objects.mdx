`ArcInvestigation`, `ArcStudy`, and `ArcAssay` are the core ISA metadata
objects. Investigations hold studies and assays. Studies register the assays
that belong to them.

## Identifiers and Titles

Identifiers are stable machine-facing names. They are used for registration
and often become part of ARC-relative paths. Titles and descriptions are
human-facing metadata and can change without changing the object identity.

## Create and Register Objects

Use the parent investigation when you want object creation and registration to
stay together. `InitStudy` adds a new study to the investigation. `InitAssay`
adds a new assay and can register it in one or more studies.

## Practical Pattern

Applications usually load or create an investigation, ensure the required
study and assay exist, update their metadata, then serialize or write through
`JsonController`, `XlsxController`, or ARC contracts.

For command-line and UI applications, keep identifiers stable and put mutable
human-facing wording into titles and descriptions. A study or assay identifier
is commonly reflected in folder names and registration lists, while a title can
be adjusted without moving files.

When you need a full ARC instead of a standalone investigation, use the `ARC`
object. It extends the same ISA model with a filesystem tree, optional license
metadata, and contract-based read/write behavior.
